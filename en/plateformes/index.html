TEMPO

The digital situation room. Setting the pace for every decision.

AIAI prepares, people decide. TEMPO extracts instructions from a record of decisions, drafts notes in which every sentence cites its source and answers the minister’s office’s questions, without ever approving in place of an authorised officer.

A single register of government instructions, linked to an approval workflow, supporting evidence and reliable indicators. Moving from a self-reported percentage to a documented status.

  • Register of instructions, actions, milestonesStructured tracking of responsibilities, deliverables and deadlines.
  • Declare, verify, approve workflowSeparate roles to make every decision reliable.
  • Supporting evidence, intact historyEvidence and earlier versions remain accessible.
  • Dashboards, frozen meeting packsA documented, dated overview to prepare decisions.
  • Reminders, escalations, delay alertsBlockers and required actions are made visible.
  • Declare ≠ verify ≠ approveThree acts, three people.
  • Missing data is not a zeroGaps are flagged.
  • History is never rewrittenPrevious values remain visible.

Guichet Unique

All public services, through a single point of contact.

A platform that brings administrative procedures, payments and local services together behind a single user account, available in branches, through an agent network and online. Already built for two countries, it adapts to each state’s service catalogue and regulatory framework.

  • Single user accountRegistration, identity verification and history of all requests.
  • Administrative proceduresSubmission, supporting documents, real-time tracking and document collection.
  • Payments and collectionsFees, bills and taxes through licensed partners.
  • Partner financial servicesAssisted account opening, transfers and services from licensed banks.
  • Mail and parcelsSending, tracking, notifications and pick-up at service points.
  • Agent network and branchesAgent terminals, cash desk, reconciliation and commissions.
  • Back office and monitoringProcessing queues, lead times, dashboards.
  • Interoperability and securityNational platforms, audit logs.

AEGIS AI

The AI assistant that acts while you talk.

Just talk to it naturally. AEGIS prepares meetings, tracks commitments and decisions, spots contradictions and delivers verified briefs. AI proposes, people decide.

  • Voice conversationIt listens, answers and launches actions during the conversation.
  • Meeting preparationOpen items, overdue commitments and a one-minute audio briefing.
  • Minutes into actionsImported transcript, actions proposed then approved by the user.
  • Commitments registerWho owes what, by when, with draft reminders ready.
  • Contradiction detectionBoth sources shown, the old value never erased.
  • Verified missionsGoal, budget and rights defined; deliverable checked before hand-over.
  • Multi-model second opinionOne model produces, another reviews; differences visible.
  • Memory and governanceViewable, editable memory; budgets and rights set by the organisation.

TelliBell

Everything about the class, in one place.

The school communication platform that brings teachers, families and school leaders together in a secure space: homework, learning progress, report cards, absences and messaging.

  • Targeted announcementsA class, a group or a family, always the right recipient.
  • Homework and tasksPosted by the teacher, visible to the family every day.
  • Learning progressGrades, progress by subject and teacher comments.
  • Report cardsMid-term and end-of-term report cards online.
  • Absences in two tapsThe family reports it, the teacher is informed instantly.
  • Private messagingDirect, confidential exchanges between families and teachers.
  • Read receiptsDelivery and read status for every message.
  • Leadership and complianceRole-based access, audit log, data export and deletion.
