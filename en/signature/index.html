Four ways to deploy.

From a ready-to-use portal to your own certification authority: choose the level of integration and sovereignty that matches your requirements.

  • Option 1

    SaaS portal

    Start signing today.

    A ready-to-use platform, hosted in a secure environment, to send, sign and track your documents with no prior integration.

    Ideal forStart-ups, schools, associations, independent professionals and business teams.

  • Option 2

    API

    Signature at the heart of your tools.

    Documented APIs to embed electronic signature in your applications, ERP or CRM. Logic Aegis designs and delivers the integration with your teams.

    Ideal forSoftware publishers, legaltech, CRM, real estate, banks.

  • Option 3

    On-premises

    Your own signature service.

    Full sovereignty: the solution runs in your infrastructure so you keep control of data and processes. Become your own signature provider.

    Ideal forLarge administrations, the banking sector, paperless processes with evidential value.

  • Option 4

    PKI consulting

    A tailored trust infrastructure.

    Design, deployment and management of your PKI: servers, HSMs, smart cards, certification policies, training and follow-up.

    Ideal forNational PKIs, certification authorities, financial institutions.
